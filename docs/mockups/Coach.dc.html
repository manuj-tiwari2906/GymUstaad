Ask the coach

Why did you add chest sets? I thought 6 was enough.
Your chest got about 6 sets a week for 8 weeks and your press barely moved. Most people grow best on about 12 to 20 sets a week, so we raised it to 14 in two steps.
Since then incline press is up 7.5 kg. That fits the change, though 4 weeks is early to be sure.
Based on: your training log, weeks 1 to 4 · Baz-Valle et al. 2022
